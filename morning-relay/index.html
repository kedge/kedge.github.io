1. Scope and local alarm data

This policy covers the Morning Relay Android app, operated by kedge. You do not need an account. Alarm times, labels, first actions, scan settings, active wake-up sessions, preferences and recent activity history are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not run a server that receives this alarm or scan data.

Activity history is limited to the most recent 30 days and at most 500 completed sessions. You can clear history in the app without deleting alarm plans. Deleting a plan removes that plan; an already active session keeps its snapshot until it finishes. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the local app data. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for this app.

2. Optional camera scanning and permissions

Barcode/QR verification is optional and off by default for each alarm. Camera permission is requested when you choose scanning. Images are processed on the device for decoding; the app does not save or upload camera frames. It stores a SHA-256 matching identifier for a bound code locally, rather than the original code text. A matching identifier can still relate to a code; please avoid binding sensitive personal codes.

Alarm, notification, full-screen notification, wake lock, vibration, boot and foreground media-playback permissions support scheduled ringing and recovery. The app does not request microphone, contacts or precise-location permission. Network access is used for consent, advertising and Google Play purchases. Advertising-ID permission may be included by the advertising SDK.

3. Advertising, consent and third-party processing

After your permission in the app, InMobi CMP determines applicable regional consent choices. InMobi advertising starts only when the app receives a resolved permission state that allows it. Declining this permission does not restrict alarm features. Advertisements appear only on the quiet home screen; ringing, scanning and follow-up screens do not display advertisements. Active subscribers do not receive advertisements.

When enabled, InMobi and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers where available, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device and app information, network information, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics including SDK crash logs, and fraud-prevention signals. These are used to deliver and measure advertising, manage consent, secure services and prevent fraud. We do not send alarm labels, schedules, scan identifiers or history to InMobi. Consent services may process network/device information to determine your region, save choices and provide consent records.

You can manage regional choices or withdraw the app's advertising permission in Settings. Withdrawal stops new ad requests in the app; it does not erase information already held by a provider. Device advertising-ID controls are also available through Android. Provider practices, partners, retention and rights are described in InMobi's Privacy Policy and InMobi's opt-out information.

4. Purchases and Google Play

Google Play processes subscriptions and payment details. The app receives product information, a purchase token, purchase state and acknowledgement state to enable or restore the ad-free entitlement. The app stores the last successfully checked entitlement locally so temporary connection failures do not immediately restore advertisements. We do not collect your card details. Google processes purchases under Google's Privacy Policy. An Internet connection and an available Google Play service are needed to purchase and verify current subscription status.

5. Retention, security and your choices

Local data remains on your device according to the controls above. InMobi and Google retain data under their respective policies and legal obligations. Android app-private storage and HTTPS are used where supported; no system can promise absolute security. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than yours, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or limit processing of personal information, withdraw consent or opt out of certain advertising uses. For local alarm information, use the app's controls or Android's clear-storage option. Contact us at the email above for privacy requests and contact the relevant provider for information it controls. Do not send payment credentials or sensitive codes in a support email.

6. Audience and updates

This app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information to us. Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date and, when necessary, an in-app notice or renewed choice.

Subscription terms

All alarm features are free. The optional Ad-free Monthly subscription removes all advertisements in the app. The US base price is US$1.99 per month. Local currency, taxes and final price are shown by Google Play before confirmation. No free trial is promised.

Payment is charged to your Google Play account upon purchase. The subscription renews monthly unless canceled through Google Play before the next renewal. Cancellation stops future renewals; access ordinarily continues until the end of the paid period. Manage or cancel in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Use Restore purchases in the app with the same Google Play account. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund rules and applicable law.

1. 适用范围与本地闹钟数据

本政策适用于 kedge 提供的 Android 应用“离床接力”（Morning Relay）。使用应用无需注册账号。闹钟时间、名称、第一件事、扫码设置、当前起床轮次、偏好与近期过程记录保存在手机的应用私有存储中。我们没有接收这些闹钟或扫码数据的服务器。

过程记录保留最近 30 天、最多 500 个已结束轮次。可在应用内清空历史而不删除计划；删除计划后，正在进行的轮次会保留快照直到结束。卸载或清除应用存储会移除本地数据。本应用已关闭 Android 云备份和设备迁移。

2. 可选扫码与权限

每个闹钟的扫码验证默认关闭，选择扫码时才申请相机权限。相机画面仅在本机解码，应用不保存或上传原始画面。绑定的条码仅以本地 SHA-256 匹配标识保存，不保存原始内容；匹配标识仍可能与具体条码关联，请不要绑定敏感个人码。

闹钟、通知、全屏提醒、唤醒、振动、开机恢复及前台媒体播放权限用于定时响铃。应用不申请麦克风、通讯录或精确定位权限。网络用于同意管理、广告及 Google Play 订阅；广告 SDK 可能合并广告标识符权限。

3. 广告与同意管理

经你在应用中允许后，InMobi CMP 提供适用地区的隐私选择；仅在同意状态已明确且允许广告时启动 InMobi 广告。拒绝不影响闹钟功能。广告仅出现在没有活动起床轮次的首页，响铃、扫码和复查页面不展示广告。有效订阅用户不展示广告。

启用后，InMobi 及其广告合作方可能处理可用的广告标识符、IP 地址及其推断的大致位置、设备和应用信息、网络信息、广告展示与互动、诊断（包括 SDK 崩溃日志）及反欺诈信息，用于广告投放与测量、同意管理和服务安全。我们不会向 InMobi 发送闹钟名称、时间、扫码匹配标识或过程记录。同意管理服务可能使用网络和设备信息判断地区、保存选择和提供同意记录。

可在“设置”管理地区隐私选项或撤回应用广告许可。撤回后停止新的广告请求，但不会自动删除服务商已持有的信息。也可使用 Android 广告标识符控制。服务商的数据处理、合作方、保留期限及权利说明见上方 InMobi 隐私政策和退出链接。
